Queen Mountain

A misty ridge adventure with sunrise views, pine winds, and quiet forest trails.

Queen Mountain Story

Queen Mountain welcomed us with cool winds, rolling mist, and a calm trail through forest and open grass patches. The climb is moderate, but the top rewards every step with wide valley views and a peaceful golden-hour sky.

This route is perfect for a one-day trek with friends. Start early, carry enough water, and stay for sunset if the weather is clear.

Quick Trek Tips

  • Best time: early morning for clear ridge views.
  • Wear shoes with good grip in wet weather.
  • Carry a light jacket; summit winds can be cold.
  • Take back all waste and keep the trail clean.
